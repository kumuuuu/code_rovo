## Brand & Style

The design system projects an aura of surgical precision, high-order engineering excellence, and quiet technological confidence. Rooted in Apple-inspired restraint blended with modern developer-centric sophistication, it targets global enterprise leaders, high-growth scale-ups, and technology innovators seeking elite offshore software craftsmanship.

The visual style unites **Dark Surface Precision** with **Atmospheric Fluid Luminosity**:

- **Tactile Depth & Subtlety**: Layered dark tones derived from deep space navy (`#07090E`) to create natural visual hierarchies without jarring contrasts.
- **Controlled Chromatic Energy**: The signature spectrum gradient (cyan through electric blue, indigo, purple, to magenta) is deployed with forensic restraint—manifesting primarily as ambient glows, hairline edge highlights, subtle active indicators, and typographic accents, never as garish saturation.
- **Editorial Calm & Generous Space**: Massive negative space, disciplined proportions, and strict typographical balance evoke confidence, authority, and architectural weight.
- **Precision Engineering Metaphors**: Delicate micro-dot grid matrices, hairline node interconnections, and optical glow blurs visually articulate complex infrastructure, systems architecture, and pristine code.

## Colors

The palette relies on a tonal hierarchy of deep obsidian-navy surfaces contrasted against high-clarity neutrals and a dynamic, fluid spectral gradient.

### Surfaces & Foundations

- **Base Canvas**: `#07090E` — Infinite abyss with deep blue-gray undertones.
- **Secondary Canvas / Recessed**: `#0D111A` — Inset sections, side rails, table headers.
- **Elevated Surfaces / Cards**: `#131824` — Interactive cards, panels, floating modals.
- **Overlay Floating Surface**: `#1A2234` — Dropdowns, tooltips, high-level popovers.

### Hairline Borders

- **Standard Border**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` / `#1E293B` — Crisp structural separation.
- **Subtle Inner Stroke**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` — Inset highlight for elevated components.
- **Active / Accent Border**: `rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.35)` to `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.35)` — Focused or highlighted interactive states.

### Fluid Accent Spectrum

The core brand signature transitions smoothly across five coordinated stops:

1. `Cyan` (`#00F0FF`) — Leading edge, high-intensity telemetry indicators, and micro accents.
2. `Electric Blue` (`#3B82F6`) — Primary interactive buttons, links, and system focal points.
3. `Violet / Indigo` (`#6366F1`) — Mid-tone harmony, focus rings, state transitions.
4. `Purple` (`#A855F7`) — Rich depth, secondary glows, category badges.
5. `Magenta` (`#EC4899`) — Terminal stop, high-priority notifications, contrast accents.

### Typography & Content Neutrals

- **High Contrast (Headings)**: `#F8FAFC` — Pure crisp readout.
- **Standard Primary (Titles, Emphasis)**: `#E2E8F0` — Clear, anti-glare white-slate.
- **Secondary Body Text**: `#94A3B8` — Balanced, low-fatigue reading gray.
- **Muted / Metadata**: `#64748B` — Disclaimers, tags, secondary metadata.
- **Ghost / Inactive**: `#334155` — Placeholders, disabled actions, track lines.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy prioritizes Swiss-modernist legibility, extreme optical tightness on headlines, and generous, fatigue-free line heights for dense technical prose.

### Optical Dynamics

- **Tightened Kerning on Scale**: As type sizes exceed 24px, negative letter-spacing dynamically scales from `-0.015em` to `-0.035em`, preventing air gaps between glyphs and producing an authoritative, editorial density reminiscent of Apple Keynote titles.
- **Airy Body Architecture**: All continuous body text utilizes a line-height ratio between `1.65` and `1.7` (`body-md` sets 15px with 25px line height), maximizing visual comfort against deep dark surfaces.
- **Code & Metric Accents**: Micro labels, metrics, and technological stamps leverage JetBrains Mono (`label-code`) in all-caps or tabular digits to reinforce engineering rigor.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors cinematic product staging: high breathing room, structured horizontal containment, and an exacting 12-column foundation.

### Grid Rhythm & Breakpoints

- **Desktop (1280px - 1440px+)**: Strict 12-column grid. Max content width of `1280px` with dynamic centering. Column gutters set to `2rem` (`gutter-lg`) with outer margin margins expanding from `3rem` to `5rem` (`margin-desktop`).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters (`gutter`) and `2rem` side margins (`margin`). Secondary side-by-side elements collapse into single-column or 2-column modules.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px)**: 4-column layout with `1rem` gutters (`gutter-sm`) and `1.25rem` side margins (`margin-mobile`). All complex multi-card groups stack vertically.

### Section Pacing & Vertical Cadence

- Major storytelling chapters separate by `space-3xl` (`6rem`) to `8rem` on desktop, enforcing deliberate pauses before introducing new service paradigms.
- Sub-components and related grouping clusters rely strictly on `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) or `space-xl` (`2.5rem`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on opaque dropshadows. It utilizes stacked tonal surfaces, frosted glass transmission, and ultra-diffused atmospheric back-glows.

### Surface Stratification

1. **Level 0 (Floor)**: `#07090E` — Pure foundation.
2. **Level 1 (Sub-section / Recessed panels)**: `#0D111A` with a 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
3. **Level 2 (Standard Elevated Cards)**: `#131824` layered with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and a top-edge highlight stroke (`border-top: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, other edges `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`).
4. **Level 3 (Interactive Hover / Floating Modals)**: `#1A2234` with an ambient glow shadow: `0 20px 50px -10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7), 0 0 30px -5px rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.15)`.

### Atmospheric Radiance & Vector Matrix

- **Diffused Under-Glows**: Under key cards and visual hero assets, apply radial gradient backdrops with `blur(80px)` using low opacity (`rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.08)` to `rgba(168, 85, 247, 0.06)`).
- **Subtle Vector Dot Matrix**: Background sections incorporate an ultra-faint SVG dot pattern (`fill="rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)"`, 24px grid spacing) masked with radial vignettes so they gently vanish into the canvas edges.
- **Node Connections**: Technical diagrams employ 1px stroke SVG paths (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) with tiny glowing 3px intersection points (`#00F0FF`).

## Shapes

The design system maintains a unified, highly polished geometry centered on soft structural rectangles and ergonomic contact points.

- **Standard Base Radii (0.5rem / 8px)**: Used for buttons, inputs, tags, and small utility triggers.
- **Elevated Card Radii (1rem / 16px to 1.5rem / 24px)**: All primary content cards, case-study previews, and service modules use between 16px and 24px corner radii, delivering an Apple-like hardware silhouette.
- **Pill Geometry (Full Rounded / 9999px)**: Reserved strictly for status badges, live availability indicators, navigation bars (dock style), and pill filter chips.

## Components

### Buttons

- **Primary Action (Spectrum Beam)**: Background of `#F8FAFC` with dark text (`#07090E`), or solid `#3B82F6` with subtle white text. Features an ambient hover glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px -2px rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.45)`). Border radius is 8px–10px; font weight is 600.
- **Secondary (Glass Frost)**: Background `#131824` with hairline border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`. On hover, the border color shifts to `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.4)` and surface lightens to `#1A2234`.
- **Tertiary / Text**: Crisp `#94A3B8` typography with no background. Shifts to `#F8FAFC` on hover with a chevron that translates right by 3px.

### Cards & Solution Panels

- **Container Structure**: Surface color `#131824` with a 16px–24px radius.
- **Border Treatment**: 1px uniform perimeter in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Hover states engage an animated or gradient-blended border transition picking up cyan-to-indigo hues (`rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.3)` to `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.3)`).
- **Internal Spacing**: Generous `space-xl` (2rem / 32px) internal padding to maintain calm pacing.

### Chips & Status Badges

- **Pill Badges**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) with a height of 28px. Background of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)` with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` border.
- **Active / Live Telemetry**: Contains a glowing 6px pulse dot (`#00F0FF` with a subtle infinite ping animation) alongside `label-caps` typography in `#E2E8F0`.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields

- **Base Canvas**: Surface `#0D111A` inset into `#131824` cards.
- **Borders & States**: 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. On focus, transitions cleanly to a 1px border `#3B82F6` accompanied by an ultra-subtle outer ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.15)`.
- **Typography**: Text renders in `#F8FAFC`, placeholder sits at `#64748B`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls

- **Geometry**: 18px dimensions with 4px radius for checkboxes, full circle for radios.
- **Visuals**: Dark background (`#0D111A`) with hairline border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`. Selected state fills with `#3B82F6` featuring an SVG white check or inner dot.

### Navigation Header (Floating Dock)

- **Execution**: Horizontally centered floating dock elevated over canvas with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`, surface `rgba(13, 17, 26, 0.75)`, and border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Border radius is 9999px (pill-shaped) with compact internal padding (8px vertical, 20px horizontal).
